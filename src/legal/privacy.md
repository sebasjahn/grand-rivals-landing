# Privacy Policy

_Last updated June 2026_

## Who we are

Grand Rivals ("we", "us") operates this prediction game. This policy explains what personal data we collect, why, and the rights you have over it, in line with the GDPR.

## What we collect

We collect only what's needed to run the game:

- Your email address (for sign-in and account security)
- Your display name and, optionally, your country
- Your predictions, scores, tokens and league memberships

## Why we collect it

We use this data solely to operate the game — to create your account, run predictions and scoring, place you in leaderboards and leagues, and keep the Service secure. We do not use it for advertising.

## How it's stored

Your data is stored with Supabase, our database and authentication provider, hosted in the EU region. Access is restricted and protected by row-level security.

## How long we keep it

We keep your data for as long as your account is active. If you delete your account, your personal data and game history are permanently removed.

## Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the right to:

- Access the personal data we hold about you
- Correct inaccurate data (you can edit your profile in the app)
- Delete your account and data — available any time from Profile → menu → Delete account

## Cookies

We use minimal, strictly functional cookies and local storage — only what's required to keep you signed in and run the app. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

## Third parties

We share data only with Supabase, as our hosting and authentication provider, to operate the Service. We do not sell your data to anyone.

## Contact

For any privacy request or question, contact us at privacy@grandrivals.com.

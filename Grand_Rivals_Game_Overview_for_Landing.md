# Grand Rivals — Game Overview (for the landing page)

This file gives you the full picture of what Grand Rivals is, so every section of the landing page is accurate and the whole site feels like it understands the product. This is marketing context — not a technical spec. Keep the site exciting and clear; never dump rules or numbers on the visitor unless a section calls for it.

---

## The one-liner
Grand Rivals is an F1 prediction game for you and your friends. Predict the top 10 finishing order of every race, score points on how accurate you are, and compete across a whole season.

## The feeling we're selling
Race-day tension. The thrill of calling a result right, the gut-punch when your pick crashes out. It's social, competitive, and runs the length of the real F1 season — every race weekend matters. It's about bragging rights in your group, not gambling.

## How a race works (the core loop)
1. Before each race, you predict the finishing order of the top 10 drivers.
2. You can optionally play one strategic token to boost or protect your score.
3. Predictions lock the moment the session starts — then everyone's picks are revealed.
4. After the race, points are scored automatically and the leaderboard updates.
5. You earn badges, climb your group, and chase the season title.

## Scoring (the tension — use real numbers only in the scoring section)
- Nail a driver's exact position: 15 points.
- One position off: 8. Two off: 4. Three or four off: nothing.
- Five or more off, or your driver crashes out: you lose 5 points.
- Big bonuses for calling the winner, the full podium, or a perfect top 10.
The point: bold correct calls win seasons, bad calls hurt, and a DNF on your pick is a real gut-punch. That risk is the fun.

## Tokens (the strategy layer) — five of them, one per race max
- Full Throttle — double your points that race.
- Safety Car — cancel any negative points that race.
- Slipstream — double down on one driver (double points or penalty).
- Purple Sector — predict the fastest lap.
- Undercut — sneak in one extra pick from outside the top 10.
Limited uses across a season, so timing matters.

## Spotlight Races (variety) — up to 4 special weekends a season
Announced at the season start, marked on the calendar. They change the format:
- The Grid — predict qualifying.
- Sprint Start — predict the sprint.
- Full Field — predict all 22 drivers.
- Pit Wall — predict the constructors.
- Wheel to Wheel — call which driver beats their teammate.

## Social (why it's fun with friends)
- Create a private group, share a join code, invite your friends. Unlimited players.
- Also compete in global, country, and favourite-team leagues with everyone.
- Leaderboards with a podium for the top 3, banter, overtakes, rivalries.

## Badges and the season arc
- Collect badges all season — from your first exact call to legendary feats like a perfect top 10.
- Predict the season's Drivers and Constructors champions for big end-of-season points (back an underdog, win bigger).
- The season ends with a shareable podium and a personal recap of your best and worst moments.

## What it costs
Free to play. No ads interrupting the race.

## Tone and language for the site
- Confident, punchy, race-engineering voice. Short lines. Precision over fluff.
- It's a game of skill and nerve among friends — never framed as betting or gambling.
- Use F1 language naturally (lights out, the grid, the podium, lap one) but never F1/team/driver logos, photos, or official branding.
